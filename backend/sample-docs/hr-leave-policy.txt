ACME CORPORATION - HR LEAVE POLICY (POL-HR-2026-V1)

=== PAGE 1 ===
1. OVERVIEW AND ANNUAL LEAVE
Acme Corporation is committed to supporting work-life balance for all regular full-time employees.
Employees accrue 18 days of paid Annual Leave per calendar year, earned at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service.
Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 business days in advance via the HR Portal and approved by the reporting manager.
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 30 unused annual leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any accrued balance exceeding 30 days on December 31st will lapse automatically without encashment.
In addition to annual leave, all employees receive 12 days of Casual Leave each calendar year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot be carried forward.

=== PAGE 2 ===
2. SICK LEAVE AND BEREAVEMENT
All full-time employees are allocated 10 days of paid Sick Leave at the beginning of each calendar year.
If an employee is absent due to illness for more than 3 consecutive working days, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HR Portal upon return.
Sick leave cannot be encashed upon separation from the company.
Employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid Bereavement Leave in the event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
For extended medical conditions lasting beyond available sick leave, an employee may request unpaid medical leave subject to HR Director approval.

=== PAGE 3 ===
3. PARENTAL LEAVE AND SABBATICAL
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid Maternity Leave for up to two surviving children. Maternity leave can commence up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery.
Male employees and primary non-birthing partners are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid Paternity Leave, which must be taken within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
Adoption leave provides 16 weeks of paid leave for adopting a child under one year of age.
Sabbatical leave: Employees who have completed at least 3 years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 3 to 12 months for advanced study, research, or public service.
Health insurance coverage remains active during approved sabbaticals, provided the employee pays the standard monthly premium contribution.
