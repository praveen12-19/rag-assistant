ACME CORPORATION - BUSINESS TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY (POL-TRV-2026-V1)

=== PAGE 1 ===
1. TRAVEL BOOKINGS AND LODGING GUIDELINES
All business travel must be pre-approved by the department manager before any reservations are made.
Flights: For domestic and international flights with scheduled duration under 6 continuous hours, employees must book economy class. For international flights exceeding 6 hours of continuous flight time, employees are eligible to book premium economy class. Business class travel is permitted only for C-suite executives or with prior written sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
Hotel accommodations: Hotel bookings must be arranged through the corporate travel partner tool (Navan).
The maximum allowable nightly lodging reimbursement is $200 per night (excluding taxes) for domestic locations, and up to $350 per night for high-cost international tier-1 cities (e.g., London, Tokyo, Zurich, New York).
Personal upgrades and hotel loyalty club fee reimbursements are the sole responsibility of the employee.

=== PAGE 2 ===
2. DAILY MEAL PER DIEM AND GROUND TRANSPORTATION
Acme Corporation provides a daily meal allowance (per diem) to cover breakfast, lunch, and dinner while on business travel status.
Domestic per diem rate: $75 per day total (suggested breakdown: $15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
International per diem rate: $120 per day total.
Alcohol policy: Alcohol purchases of any kind are strictly non-reimbursable and must be paid for separately from business meal receipts.
Client entertainment dinners where alcohol is served must be pre-authorized by a Vice President and submitted under Client Entertainment rather than individual travel meals.
Ground transportation: Employees should utilize standard rideshare services (UberX, Lyft Standard) or public transit. Premium rideshare tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) and rental cars above mid-size class are not reimbursable without prior manager justification.

=== PAGE 3 ===
3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT AND APPROVAL WORKFLOW
All business travel expense reports must be submitted via Concur within 14 calendar days following the conclusion of the trip.
Itemized receipts are mandatory for all individual expenses of $25 or greater. Credit card summary slips that do not display purchased items line-by-line are not acceptable for audit compliance.
Approval matrix:
- Expense reports up to $1,000 require approval by the direct line Manager.
- Expense reports between $1,001 and $5,000 require approval by the Department Vice President (VP).
- Expense reports exceeding $5,000 require approval by both the VP and the Corporate Controller.
Reimbursements are processed via direct deposit on the standard bi-weekly payroll cycle after final audit approval.
Unused airline credits or hotel vouchers issued due to company trip cancellations remain the property of Acme Corporation and must be applied toward future business travel.
